# Natural research imagery

Mode: built-in image_gen. These are generated illustrative spaces, not photographs of MACS Lab or actual clinical results.

## overview-natural-v1.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: university research laboratory website panoramic hero background, wide 3:1 composition. A quiet, believable editorial photograph of an ordinary contemporary Korean university computing research space. Natural window light, soft neutral daylight, matte materials, subtle warm wood and off-white walls, muted colors, restrained contrast, realistic imperfections and unstyled objects. Composition: camera across a room or long desk, subjects mostly in RIGHT half; LEFT half a softly shaded out-of-focus real interior area, quiet enough for white HTML text. Authentic documentary photography, 35mm lens, realistic geometry and practical equipment. No people, no legible text, logos, watermarks, medical patient data, glass sculptures, holograms, glowing circuitry, staged futuristic laboratory or artificial blue lighting. This is illustrative space imagery, not a photograph claiming to depict a particular existing laboratory. Subject: a sunlit university computational research workspace with a long wood desk, two monitors with subdued unreadable scientific windows, a few books and notebooks, a modest green plant and a large window with soft trees outside. Wide environmental view, welcoming and lived-in, not a luxury office.
