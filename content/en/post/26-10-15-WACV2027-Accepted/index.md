We are pleased to announce that **research first-authored by MACS Lab undergraduate researcher Jun Ho Lee** has been accepted to the **Applications Track** of the **IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision 2027 (WACV 2027)**.

**"Learning When to Correct: Selective Calibration for Monocular Kinematics"** introduces a selective calibration method that learns when to correct joint angles estimated from monocular video. To reduce overcorrection of already accurate estimates, the method decides whether to preserve the incumbent estimate or adopt an ExtraTrees correction.

Evaluated across 82 subjects, four capture domains, and four pose estimators, the method achieves a macro MAE of **5.94°** and reduces preservation failure at a 5° tolerance from **34.7% to 16.5%**.

The authors are **Jun Ho Lee (undergraduate researcher and first author)**, **Dongjun Kang**, and **Kyungsu Lee**. Congratulations to Jun Ho Lee and his coauthors on this international conference acceptance and a meaningful research achievement during his undergraduate studies.
